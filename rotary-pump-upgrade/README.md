# Upgrading To a Rotary Pump
This is general instructions, parts lists, and diagrams for upgrading the Oscar II to a Rotary Pump Design.

While the video shows how you might fit it INTO the Oscar II, I will be taking his final approach of installing it under a counter, as this Oscar II is part of a Mobile Coffee Cart.
